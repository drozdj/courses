# Exploring the Ethical Implications of Foundation Models

---
- **Type:** Essay and Short Code Implementation
- **Description:** Foundation Models have immense potential, but also raise significant ethical concerns. This assignment requires you to:    
    - **Essay:** Write an essay analyzing the ethical implications of a specific application of foundation models (e.g., biased language generation, deepfakes, automated decision-making). Discuss potential harms, mitigation strategies, and the role of researchers and developers in ensuring responsible development.
    - **Implementation:** Implement a simple bias detection/mitigation technique on a pre-trained language model. Document your findings and discuss the limitations of your approach.
        
- **Success Criteria:**
    - **Essay:** Clear articulation of ethical concerns, well-supported arguments, insightful analysis, and consideration of multiple perspectives. Demonstrate understanding of relevant ethical frameworks.
    - **Implementation:** Functional implementation of the chosen bias detection/mitigation technique. Clear documentation of the code and results. Thoughtful discussion of limitations.
